# DSA-113 — `Educacao`, light: the primary label over a gray surface

## Goal
Before this task, in the brand `Educacao`, light, `color/primary-on-surface` was `theme/brand-600`, `#99651b`. Measured by WCAG, it was 4.96:1 over `color/background` (`#ffffff`) and 4.64:1 over `color/primary-surface` (`#fff6ea`), the background of the primary outline and light; but 4.27:1 over `color/muted` (`#edeeef`), the case of the primary ghost `nph-button`, with no background of its own, on a gray card. Only that case was below the 4.5:1 of WCAG 1.4.3 for normal text. The design owner decided to raise it to 4.5:1 or more (item 7).

## Where the decision is
In the evidence of the `foundation-decision` gate: `docs/operacao/evidencias/DSA-113/foundation-decision-2026-11-23.md`. The `nph-split-button` found it (Pend. 14, decided as item 7); the color comes from the approved `nph-button` by instance, and the button alone fails the same way.

## What changes
- **First, the tone (the design owner): answered on 2026-11-23.** Option 2, only what `color/primary-on-surface` points to in `Educacao` light; the token goes to `v/5.0.0` in the token commit of this task (see "Decision answered on 2026-11-23").
- **Then, the code owner:** the contrast figures that cite the pair in the specs (the `nph-button` says the lowest value is 4.64:1, in primary outline and light of the light scheme) and in `design.md` are measured again and updated; the contrast tests, if any, cover the primary ghost over `color/muted`.

## Decision answered on 2026-11-23
The design owner chose **option 2** of the Figma preview (each option shown in light and dark): in the brand `Educacao`, light scheme, only `color/primary-on-surface` takes a darker step, `core/educacao/800` (`#664412`). The solid button (`color/primary`), the link (`color/link`) and the border (`color/primary-border`) stay on `theme/brand-600` (`#99651b`). The dark scheme and the other six brands do not change. The refused options changed the whole brand: 1, `#91601a` (4.64:1 over `color/muted`), and 3, `#8c5d19` (4.89:1).

- **Token (token rule T9, no new value in the core).** A new theme token, `theme/brand-text-light`, the light pair of `theme/brand-text-dark`: `core/educacao/800` in `Educacao`, and the same step as `theme/brand-600` (`core/<vertical>/600`) in the other six brands. `color/primary-on-surface` aliases it in light; dark stays on `theme/brand-100`. Read variable by variable in Figma: across every semantic token, both schemes and the seven brands, only `Educacao` light changed value, in `color/primary-on-surface` and in the component tokens that alias it (in this repository: `toolbar/bulk-count-foreground`, `notification/neutral-action-foreground`, `tabs/button-light-foreground-active`, `tabs/button-outline-foreground-active`, `stepper/marker-foreground-complete`, `file-upload/icon-foreground-dragging`, `file-upload/foreground-dragging`, `scheduler/event-foreground`, `scheduler/event-dot-selected`, `scheduler/day-selected-foreground`, `card/icon-container-foreground`, `timeline/marker-foreground`, `timeline/marker-foreground-primary` and `accordion/tile-foreground`).
- **Contrast (WCAG formula).** `#664412` measures 7.53:1 over `color/muted` (`#edeeef`), 8.75:1 over `color/background` (`#ffffff`) and 8.17:1 over `color/primary-surface` (`#fff6ea`); before, `#99651b` measured 4.27:1, 4.96:1 and 4.64:1. The dark label of the primary ghost `nph-button` is unchanged (`#ffeed5` over `#2d333b`, 11.19:1).
- **Why a separate text step (market research).** None of the systems read darkens the whole brand; they separate the text step from the solid fill step, and warm light hues take a darker step or black text:
  - Radix Colors, steps 11 and 12 for text, 9 and 10 for solid fills: https://www.radix-ui.com/colors/docs/palette-composition/understanding-the-scale
  - Adobe Spectrum, text and icon tokens with guaranteed contrast: https://spectrum.adobe.com/page/color-system/
  - GOV.UK, a link token darker than the brand: https://design-system.service.gov.uk/styles/colour/
  - Atlassian, tokens named by property, with their own inverse warning tokens to pass AA: https://atlassian.design/foundations/color
  - Primer, foreground colors separate from emphasis backgrounds: https://primer.style/foundations/color/overview
  - WCAG 1.4.3, no rounding and a margin above the minimum: https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/contrast-minimum.html
- **Still with the code owner.** The contrast figures that cite the pair in the specs (the `nph-button` lowest value of 4.64:1) and in `design.md` (the `use` text of `color/primary-on-surface` and of the tokens that cite 4.64:1 or 4.78:1 in light) are measured again and updated by the code owner, with the contrast tests, if any. The token commit changes only the token sources, the generated CSS, the entry of `theme/brand-text-light` and the light line of `color/primary-on-surface` in `design.md`.

## How it is proved
**`foundation-decision`** — passed. **`review-and-merge`** — the label of the primary ghost `nph-button` over `color/muted` in `Educacao` light measures 4.5:1 or more; no other pair of the brand drops below its minimum; the proof commands in `docs/operacao/component-handoff.md` exit 0; merged into `v/5.0.0`.

## What this task does not do
It does not change the dark scheme or another brand. It does not change the `nph-split-button` (DSA-109), which takes the color from the `nph-button`.
