# DSA-113 — `Educacao`, light: the primary label over a gray surface

## Goal
In the brand `Educacao`, light, `color/primary-on-surface` is `theme/brand-600`, `#99651b`. Measured by WCAG, it is 4.96:1 over `color/background` (`#ffffff`) and 4.64:1 over `color/primary-surface` (`#fff6ea`), the background of the primary outline and light; but 4.27:1 over `color/muted` (`#edeeef`), the case of the primary ghost `nph-button`, with no background of its own, on a gray card. Only that case is below the 4.5:1 of WCAG 1.4.3 for normal text. The design owner decided to raise it to 4.5:1 or more (item 7).

## Where the decision is
In the evidence of the `foundation-decision` gate: `docs/operacao/evidencias/DSA-113/foundation-decision-2026-11-23.md`. The `nph-split-button` found it (Pend. 14); the color comes from the approved `nph-button` by instance, and the button alone fails the same way.

## What changes
- **First, the tone (blocker B1, the design owner).** Changing the tone of a brand changes every component that uses the pair; the measure was made only on the button label. The tone is chosen in Figma, with a preview in light, and taken to the tokens by the token rule (T9; a new value enters the core first). Whether the brand-600 of `Educacao` changes (the whole primary of the brand) or only what `color/primary-on-surface` points to in that brand is part of that choice.
- **Then, the code owner:** the contrast figures that cite the pair in the specs (the `nph-button` says the lowest value is 4.64:1, in primary outline and light of the light scheme) and in `design.md` are measured again and updated; the contrast tests, if any, cover the primary ghost over `color/muted`.

## How it is proved
**`foundation-decision`** — passed. **`review-and-merge`** — the label of the primary ghost `nph-button` over `color/muted` in `Educacao` light measures 4.5:1 or more; no other pair of the brand drops below its minimum; the proof commands in `docs/operacao/component-handoff.md` exit 0; merged into `v/5.0.0`.

## What this task does not do
It does not change the dark scheme or another brand. It does not change the `nph-split-button` (DSA-109), which takes the color from the `nph-button`.
