# DSA-122 — nph-scheduler: the compact month day at 10 px

## Goal
On 2026-11-17, in the approval round of the nph-date-picker, the design owner chose one corner for
the day: the nph-scheduler day goes from 6 to 10 px, the same as the day of the nph-calendar grid
(`calendar/day-radius`, 10). She saw the preview in Figma (7139:33787, option A 10 px, option B 6 px).
This task brings the code to it.

## What changed in the tokens (on v/5.0.0 in the same push as this task)
- `scheduler/day-radius`: `radius/control` (6) → `core/radius/450` (10), straight to core by decision.
  `radius/control` is the default of what is operated and would leave two corners for the same piece;
  `radius/overlay` (10) is a large floating layer and `focus/radius-control` (10) is the focus halo of
  a control. The `origin` of `semantic.tokens.json` and `design.md` (`direto_por_decisao` of the
  radius scale, the `radius/control` use and the table of radius roles) say why.
- Generated: `--nph-scheduler-day-radius: var(--nph-core-radius-450)`.
- Added on 2026-11-26: `scheduler/day-focus-border-radius` → `core/radius/off-scale/11` and
  `scheduler/day-focus-halo-radius` → `core/radius/off-scale/15`, straight to core by decision: the
  semantic tokens of 11 and 15 are the focus of other bases (`focus/radius-control-with-border`, the halo
  of a control; `focus/border-radius-surface`, the border around a card). Generated
  `--nph-scheduler-day-focus-border-radius` and `--nph-scheduler-day-focus-halo-radius`.

## What changes in the code
- `.grid-cell` (compact month day): no change in the CSS; it already reads
  `--nph-scheduler-day-radius`, which is now 10 px.
- `.day` (week day header) and `.cell` (desktop month cell): stop reading
  `--nph-scheduler-day-radius` and take no corner, as Figma draws them. This fixes an older gap
  between the code (6 px) and Figma (0); it is not a new design. Until this task is done, the token
  push shows them at 10 px.
- Focus of the compact month day (`.grid-cell`): the focus border reads
  `--nph-scheduler-day-focus-border-radius` (11 px) and the halo `--nph-scheduler-day-focus-halo-radius`
  (15 px), so the ring follows the 10 px day (corner + 1 of the border; + 4 of the halo). The other parts
  keep the focus tokens they read today (`scheduler/focus-border-radius`, 7, and
  `scheduler/focus-halo-radius`, 11). Figma draws no focus state on the phone view; the corners come from
  the design owner's choice in the preview 8439:107270.
- Test `src/components/nph-scheduler/nph-scheduler.test.ts`, line 1685, compares the chosen day corner
  of the compact month (`.grid-cell`) with the resolved token; it follows the token and needs no change.
- Sheet `fichas/nph-scheduler.md`: the token list of the day (line 493) keeps `scheduler/day-radius`;
  say that only the compact month day reads it.

## How it is proved
- The nph-scheduler stories in light and dark at the phone width: the compact month day, today and
  chosen, has a 10 px corner; the week view and the desktop month have square day header and cells.
- `npm test`: every nph-scheduler test passes. On the token push (base 04486875, code unchanged) the
  whole suite ran with 15 failures in 11 files, none in nph-scheduler and none on the two changed
  tokens: token contracts of DSA-119 and DSA-120 (for example `--nph-collapsible-content-foreground`
  not yet consumed), motion and timing tests, and the nph-notification stories and touch target.

## What this task does not do
It does not create or change a token. It does not change Figma. It does not change the focus corners
of the week day header or of the desktop month cell. It does not touch the mini month (nph-calendar grid) or the event corner
(`scheduler/event-radius`).
