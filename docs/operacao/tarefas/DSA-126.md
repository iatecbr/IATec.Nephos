# DSA-126 — nph-sidebar: corner of the floating panel option

## Goal
The floating panel of the nph-sidebar has a corner of 8 (`sidebar/flyout-radius` → `radius/overlay-anchored`),
but its options still use the corner of the fixed menu item, 12 (`sidebar/item-radius`): the option is
rounder than the panel that holds it. On 2026-11-26 the design owner chose a token of its own for the option,
with 8, the same as the panel. The nested radius rule of `design.md` would give 0 (8 − 8 of padding); 8 is a
declared exception to that rule, written in `design.md` (`raio_regras.aninhado`).

## What changed in the tokens (on v/5.0.0 in the same push as this task)
- `sidebar/flyout-item-radius` → `radius/inset` (8). Generated `--nph-sidebar-flyout-item-radius`.
- `sidebar/flyout-item-focus-border-radius` → `focus/border-radius-container` (9), the corner + 1.
  Generated `--nph-sidebar-flyout-item-focus-border-radius`.
- `sidebar/flyout-item-focus-halo-radius` → `focus/radius-container` (13), the corner + 5.
  Generated `--nph-sidebar-flyout-item-focus-halo-radius`.

## What Figma draws (set `4605:9599` `nph-sidebar-flyout-option`, page "NPH — Sidebar" `1367:2`, 2026-11-26)
- The 11 variants (rest, hover, focus, active, disabled; with and without children; open) have the corner
  `sidebar/flyout-item-radius` (8); the hover and active background follow it.
- In the two focus variants, the `borda de foco` layer uses `sidebar/flyout-item-focus-border-radius` (9)
  and the `halo de foco` layer `sidebar/flyout-item-focus-halo-radius` (13).
- The same set is the option of the 2nd level (collapsed) and of the 3rd level: one token covers both.
- The item, subitem and rail button of the fixed menu are unchanged (12, focus 13 and 17).

## What changes in the code
- `src/components/nph-sidebar/nph-sidebar.css`, `.option`: `border-radius` reads
  `--nph-sidebar-flyout-item-radius` in place of `--nph-sidebar-item-radius`.
- `.option:focus-visible::before`: `border-radius` reads `--nph-sidebar-flyout-item-focus-border-radius`.
- `.option:focus-visible::after`: `border-radius` reads `--nph-sidebar-flyout-item-focus-halo-radius`.
- The `.flyout` padding and sizes do not change.

## How it is proved
- Stories in light and dark: the collapsed menu with the 2nd level open and the 3rd level open, an option in
  hover, an active option and an option with keyboard focus.
- `npm test` for nph-sidebar.

## What this task does not do
It does not create or change a token. It does not change Figma. It does not change the corner of the item,
subitem or rail button of the fixed menu, nor the panel corner.
