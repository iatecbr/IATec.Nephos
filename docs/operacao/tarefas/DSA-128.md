# DSA-128 — rich option of the select list: parts at the top

## Goal
DSA-91 D-4 drops the 20 px icon and the first 20 px line of text by 2 px, so they center on a 24 px control.
On 2026-11-26 the design owner extended it: the nph-rich-option card keeps one offset for icon and text, and
the rich option of the nph-select and nph-combobox list leaves the vertical center and aligns to the top, as
Primer ActionList and Polaris OptionList do.

## What changed in the tokens (on v/5.0.0 in the same push as this task)
- `rich-option/text-offset` was renamed `rich-option/content-offset` (same value, `core/space/50`, 2).
  Generated `--nph-rich-option-content-offset`; `--nph-rich-option-text-offset` no longer exists. No code reads it.
- New `select/option-content-offset` → `core/space/50` (2), straight to core by the declared exception of
  `design.md` (`meio_passo_use`). Generated `--nph-select-option-content-offset`.

## What Figma draws (2026-11-26)
- Card set `6910:6828` (and the older `774:154`): the icon and the `textos` block sit in one `conteúdo` wrapper
  with `rich-option/content-offset` on top. The icon and the title are 18 px from the top of the card (padding 16 + 2).
- List set `6910:7093` (40 variants): the option aligns to the top. The checkbox (`marker=checkbox`) and the
  avatar stay at the top. The start icon or dot, the texts and the check sit in one `conteúdo` wrapper with
  `select/option-content-offset` on top; the check sits in a box as tall as `select/option-icon-size` (20),
  centered, so it is centered on the first line.
- Heights: one line with icon or dot, 36 → 38; with a description, 56 → 58; with avatar or checkbox and no
  description, 40 (unchanged).

## What changes in the code
- `src/components/nph-option/nph-option.rich.css`: in the rich look, the parts align to the top
  (`align-items: flex-start` in place of center). Start icon, dot box, texts and check drop by
  `--nph-select-option-content-offset`; the check is centered in a `--nph-select-option-icon-size` box;
  the avatar and the checkbox do not drop. The plain nph-option does not change.
- `src/components/nph-rich-option/nph-rich-option.css`: `.icon` `margin-block-start` and `.texts`
  `padding-block-start` read `--nph-rich-option-content-offset` in place of the calculation (same 2 px).
- The nph-option and nph-rich-option sheets: the alignment and the new token.

## How it is proved
- Stories in light and dark: rich options with icon, dot, avatar and checkbox, with and without description,
  selected with the check; one option with a long description of two lines.
- `npm test` for nph-option and nph-rich-option.

## What this task does not do
It does not create or change a token. It does not change Figma. It does not change the plain nph-select and
nph-combobox option.
