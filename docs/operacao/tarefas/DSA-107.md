# DSA-107 — `nph-textarea` on `layout/multiline-*`, and its footer gap

## Goal
Since DSA-105, the general semantics `layout/multiline-min-height` (76) and `layout/multiline-max-height` (216) are on `v/5.0.0`, with the same values as `layout/textarea-min-height` and `layout/textarea-max-height`. The `nph-textarea` moves to the general ones, so both multi-line fields of the family read the same tokens. The heights change nothing on screen. The gap between its footer actions goes from 8 to 12 px (D-1, answered on 2026-11-23).

## Where the decision is
In the evidence of the `foundation-decision` gate: `docs/operacao/evidencias/DSA-107/foundation-decision-2026-11-22.md`. There is no need to open Figma.

## What changes
### Heights
- `src/components/nph-textarea/nph-textarea.css`: the field `min-block-size` and `max-block-size` read `--nph-layout-multiline-min-height` and `--nph-layout-multiline-max-height`, and the comment above names them.
- `src/components/nph-textarea/nph-textarea.test.ts`: the height tests and the token list read the new names; the test that checks `design.md` for `layout/textarea-*` follows the text below.
- `fichas/nph-textarea.md` and `src/shared/metadata/nph-textarea.json`: every place that names `layout/textarea-min-height` or `layout/textarea-max-height` names the new tokens.
- `design.md`: `layout/multiline-min-height` and `layout/multiline-max-height` gain the `nph-textarea` in their `use`; `layout/textarea-min-height` and `layout/textarea-max-height` stay, unchanged, and are no longer read by a piece.

Before closing, search the repository for `layout/textarea-min-height`, `layout/textarea-max-height` and `--nph-layout-textarea-m`. Change the living texts too: `nph-textarea.ts`, the demo, the stories and `.storybook/i18n/*.json` where they name the tokens. Leave as they are: `docs/operacao/evidencias/` (evidence is history), `docs/decisoes-tecnicas.md` and the old tasks, such as DSA-38 (history), and the token sources, where `layout/textarea-*` stay.

### Footer gap (D-1, answered on 2026-11-23)
The `.actions` gap reads `--nph-space-inline-loose` (12) instead of `--nph-space-inline` (8), and the spec, the metadata and the footer test follow. The `use` of `space/inline-loose` in `design.md` already names the footer of the `nph-textarea` (it came with the D-1 answer).

## Decision answered on 2026-11-23
Indiane chose the recommended option 1 of the Figma preview (draft page, light and dark, the `nph-textarea` beside the `nph-rich-text-editor`), after market research with sources.

| ID | Answer | What the code does |
|---|---|---|
| D-1 | Option 1: the footer actions take `space/inline-loose` (12 px), as the `nph-rich-text-editor`. The description of `space/inline-loose` already says "Space between the actions of a footer", and the `nph-textarea` was the only exception in the family. The market mostly puts 8 px between actions (Material 3 and GitLab Pajamas: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/material-components/material-web/main/dialog/internal/_dialog.scss , https://design.gitlab.com/components/button ; Primer and Atlassian in their dialogs: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/primer/react/main/packages/react/src/Dialog/Dialog.module.css , https://unpkg.com/@atlaskit/modal-dialog@latest/dist/es2019/modal-footer.compiled.css ), and Carbon puts 16 (https://carbondesignsystem.com/components/button/usage/ ); 12 sits in that range and follows the Nephos rule for every footer of actions. | What "Footer gap" says. |

## How it is proved
**`foundation-decision`** — passed. **`review-and-merge`** — the field still measures 76 empty and stops at 216, the footer actions are 12 px apart, the proof commands in `docs/operacao/component-handoff.md` exit 0, and the work is merged into `v/5.0.0`.

## What this task does not do
It does not create a token or change a token value. It does not remove `layout/textarea-min-height` or `layout/textarea-max-height`. It does not change the `nph-rich-text-editor` (DSA-105). It does not change Figma: the Figma `nph-textarea` footer already has 12 px between its actions (2026-11-23), and binding its heights to the new semantics is design work apart from this task.
