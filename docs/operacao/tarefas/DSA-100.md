# DSA-100 — nph-toast-region, the region that places and stacks the toasts

## Goal
The `nph-toast-region` in `src/components/nph-toast-region/`, with CSS, tests, stories and the spec `fichas/nph-toast-region.md`, in the contract accepted in Figma: the fixed region of the screen that receives the toasts of the `nph-notification` (`placement="toast"`) and decides where they appear, in what order and with what space between them. It does not draw the toast: severity, icon, text, actions and close stay with the `nph-notification`. It answers D-1 of DSA-63: the region becomes a piece of its own.

## Where the contract is
In the evidence of the `figma-docs-accepted` gate: `docs/operacao/evidencias/DSA-100/figma-docs-accepted-2026-11-22.md`. It carries the Figma documentation converted to text, with the 5 tokens, the anatomy read in Figma, the default behavior, the combined states, the inherited decisions, the icon rules, the limits, what the region asks of other pieces and the decisions answered. There is no need to open Figma.

## Specification to follow closely
- **Tokens**: bind every value of the region to its `toast-region/*` token, never to the semantic or the core it points to. The curves `motion/enter-easing` and `motion/exit-easing` and the reduced motion duration `motion/hover-duration` are used straight, as in `nph-hover-card` (P112).
- **Region**: a vertical column, no background, no border, no shadow, no radius and no clipping. Gap `toast-region/gap` between one toast and the next, only space, no divider. Toasts aligned by the end edge: toasts of different widths (stacked 360, single-line 440) end on the same vertical line.
- **Position**: in the top layer of the browser, as the `nph-tooltip` balloon (P77: no z-index, no layer token). From 768 px (the default screen), at the bottom-right corner, `toast-region/inset` from the bottom edge and from the end edge; the region takes the width of its toasts. Below 768 px (the compact screen), at the base from side to side, `toast-region/inset-compact` from the bottom and from each side; the toast takes the width of the region, even below 320 px (it replaces the minimum of 320 px of the DSA-63 evidence, P4). The region reserves no space in the page. In right-to-left, the end edge is the left one.
- **Order**: from the oldest, at the top, to the newest, at the bottom, next to the corner. The new one enters at the bottom and the earlier ones move up.
- **Quantity and queue** (item 4): at most 3 toasts in view. The 4th and the next ones wait in a queue, in order of arrival, out of view and not announced, and enter when a toast leaves. Nothing is drawn for the queue. With 3 toasts that do not close by themselves, the queue waits for the person to close one.
- **Role and announcement** (item 5): the region is `role="region"` with a name given by the application, already translated ("Notifications"), as the label that names the landmark in P109 item 5. It mounts, even when empty, two hidden live regions, one polite and one assertive; error and warning are announced in the assertive one, the others in the polite one, with the heading and the description of the toast. The toast inside the region has no role of its own, so it is not announced twice. The inline `nph-notification` keeps its role (P108 item 5) as it is.
- **Keyboard** (item 8): F6 takes the focus to the first control (action or close) of the newest toast that has a control; Tab and Shift+Tab go through the controls of the toasts; F6 again returns the focus to where it came from. With the focus inside a toast, Esc closes that toast; with the focus on the icon-only close, the first Esc closes the tooltip balloon and the second closes the toast (DSA-43 D2). Outside the region, Esc does nothing to it. When the toast that had the focus leaves, the focus goes to the next toast that has a control or, with none, back to where it came from. The toast never takes the focus by itself. The region takes no focus and has no ring of its own: the rings are those of the `nph-button` and of the `nph-icon-button`.
- **Time** (items 6 and 7): each toast keeps its own pause (P108 item 4). With the pointer on the region or the focus inside it, every toast in view pauses together and goes on with what was left when they leave. With a modal dialog open, the countdown of every toast is suspended and goes on when the dialog closes. Error, loading and a toast with an action never close by themselves.
- **Modal dialog** (Pend. 10, item 7): with a modal dialog open, the region stays below its scrim (`overlay/scrim`), in view but inert: no click, no focus and out of the accessibility tree, until the dialog closes. A toast that arrives at that moment is shown in the region, below the scrim, and announced at once through a hidden live region inside the open dialog.
- **Motion**: the toast enters in `toast-region/enter-duration` with `motion/enter-easing` and leaves in `toast-region/exit-duration` with `motion/exit-easing`, animating only opacity and transform. With reduced motion, the slide becomes only opacity in `motion/hover-duration`.
- **Clipping of the toast** (item 10): the focus ring of the action and of the close comes out whole. The `.content` and `.actions` of the `nph-notification` have no `overflow` today; keep it so, and a test proves the ring is not cut inside the region. Long text, if it must be cut, is cut only on the text layer.
- **What needs an answer** (item 9): the region carries only notices. The spec sends what needs an answer before going on to the `nph-dialog` while the `nph-alert-dialog` is deferred.

## Open points for the code
- Event, aria, slot, property and console warning, following the APG and the Custom Elements Manifest.
- **API**: how the application gives the toasts to the region. The research of 2026-11-22 recommends a declarative container: the application appends `<nph-notification placement="toast">` as a child and removes it on `nph-close`; the region watches its children (`slotchange`), applies the limit of 3 and the queue, and keeps the 4th on hidden and `inert` until there is room, as the `data-limited` of Base UI. An imperative shortcut could come later on top of it. Sonner, Mantine and React Aria are imperative; Radix is declarative; Base UI has both. The spec names the API. Sources: https://www.radix-ui.com/primitives/docs/components/toast , https://base-ui.com/react/components/toast , https://react-aria.adobe.com/Toast , https://mantine.dev/x/notifications/
- **The role of the toast inside the region**: the `nph-notification` always sets `role="alert"` or `role="status"` when it is valid (`applySemantics`). How it drops the role inside the region (it detects the region, or the region tells it) is the code owner's call. The inline toast keeps its role.
- **Pause from the region**: the timer of the `nph-notification` is private and pauses only with the pointer over the toast itself or the focus inside it. How the region pauses every toast (pointer on the region, focus inside it, an open modal dialog) and lets each one go on with what was left is the code owner's call. The spec names it.
- **Esc and the event source**: the `nph-notification` has no Esc handling, and `nph-close` carries `detail.source` `close-button` or `timeout`. Who listens to Esc (the region or the toast) and the source is the code owner's call; the research of 2026-11-22 recommends `escape`, the word `nph-dialog` already uses (`NPH_DIALOG_CLOSE_SOURCES`), and closing only the focused toast, as Radix. Fluent closes every toast on Esc; Sonner only collapses the stack. Sources: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/radix-ui/primitives/main/packages/react/toast/src/toast.tsx , https://raw.githubusercontent.com/microsoft/fluentui/master/packages/react-components/react-toast/library/src/components/Toaster/useToaster.tsx
- **Toast width on the compact screen**: the toast is `inline-size: var(--nph-notification-toast-width)` with `max-inline-size: min(100%, …toast-width-max)`, so it shrinks inside a narrower region but does not stretch on a compact screen wider than 392 px. How the toast takes the width of the region is the code owner's call; the look does not change.
- **The announcement inside an open dialog**: the `nph-dialog` has no live region (its sheet lists the default and the footer slots). Where the hidden live region lives while a modal dialog is open, and how it leaves when the dialog closes, is the code owner's call; nothing changes in the dialog's look or content.
- **Order in the top layer**: the region is a top layer element and a modal dialog opened after it goes above it. A region shown, or shown again, while a dialog is open would go above the scrim (no source read for the region; the order follows the order of opening). The code keeps the toast below the scrim of any open modal dialog.
- **Window lower than the stack** (D-1, for the design owner): until the answer, follow the recommendation of D-1 (a height limit of the window less the insets, vertical scroll kept at the bottom, the list focusable when it scrolls). It does not block the task.
- **The answer to DSA-63 D-1**: DSA-63 is `done` and keeps D-1 in `pending_decisions`. When this piece is merged, the code owner closes that D-1 with this task.

## What changes in the nph-notification with this task
- The sheet `fichas/nph-notification.md`, section "The toast region", and P108 item 10 ("the toast region is not a piece") move to the `nph-toast-region`: the region is a piece now, `role="region"` with a name instead of `role="group"`, the gap in `toast-region/gap` instead of `notification/stack-gap`, the distance from the corner in `toast-region/inset` and `toast-region/inset-compact` instead of `layout/margin-compact`, and the queue held by the region. The `ai_hints` line that says to build the region in the application's own container, and the `region` token line, follow.
- P108 item 4 gains the pause of the whole region and the suspension under a modal dialog; P108 item 5 changes only for the toast inside the region.
- The Storybook "Region" stories of the `nph-notification` (`nph-notification.stories.ts`, `nph-notification.docs.stories.ts` and their translations) become the stories of the `nph-toast-region`, or point to them.
- The bridge of the DSA-63 evidence ("It does not replace the alert dialog") reads `nph-dialog` while the `nph-alert-dialog` is deferred (item 9).
- After that, no CSS and no story reads `notification/stack-gap`; it leaves in a foundation task (item 11).

## Why it is ready
The `toast-region/*` tokens came with this task and are on `v/5.0.0`: 3 alias semantics, and `toast-region/inset` and `toast-region/inset-compact` point straight to the core by decision (the evidence says why). No new value entered the core; no semantic changed value or description. Every piece it uses is done: `nph-icon` (DSA-03), `nph-spinner` (DSA-09), `nph-button` (DSA-13), the icon-only button (DSA-43), `nph-dialog` (DSA-44), the `nph-tooltip` top layer and balloon (DSA-48), `nph-notification` (DSA-63). D-1 does not block.
