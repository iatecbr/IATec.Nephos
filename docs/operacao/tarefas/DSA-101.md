# DSA-101 — nph-chart, data chart inside a card

## Goal
The `nph-chart` in `src/components/nph-chart/`, with CSS, tests, stories and the spec `fichas/nph-chart.md`, in the contract accepted in Figma: one measure across categories, over time or in parts of a whole, inside an `nph-card`, for the person to compare, see the trend and find the point out of pattern without reading number by number. The types are column (default), horizontal bar, grouped vertical, grouped horizontal, stacked vertical, stacked horizontal, line, area, donut and `sparkline`; the area states are default, loading, empty, no result and error. The pieces are the legend item, the marker, the highlight, the data tooltip and the error state. "View as table" swaps the area for the `nph-table` with the same data.

## Where the contract is
In the evidence of the `figma-docs-accepted` gate: `docs/operacao/evidencias/DSA-101/figma-docs-accepted-2026-11-22.md`. It carries the Figma documentation converted to text, with the 65 `chart/*` tokens of the component, the anatomy part by part (with the token, the auto layout direction and the text style of each layer), the default behavior, the combined states, the icon and image rules, the limits, the rules the set does not draw, what the chart asks of other pieces, the inherited decisions and the decisions answered. There is no need to open Figma.

## Specification to follow closely
- **Parts and tokens**: as the anatomy of the evidence. Bind every value to its `chart/*` token: the 65 tokens of the component, or the foundation color tokens the evidence names (`chart/1` to `chart/6`, `chart/N-soft`, `chart/grid`, `chart/axis`, `chart/reference`, `chart/muted`, `chart/empty`), never to the semantic or the core a token points to. Text styles as the anatomy.
- **Frame and composition**: the chart lives in the content zone of an `nph-card` base, elevated or outlined, and takes its width; the card holds the title, the description with the unit (and the period when there is no selector) and the period selector in the header action. The chart is never used loose on the page. The card is not clickable.
- **Plot area**: reference height `chart/plot-height` (320); the height follows the container, the width too (560 in Figma is an example width). Vertical types: Y-axis labels on the left, right aligned, `chart/y-label-gap` from the area; horizontal grid only, `chart/grid-width` in `chart/grid`; X axis at zero, `chart/axis-width` in `chart/axis`; X-axis labels below, centered on the category, `chart/x-label-gap` away. Horizontal types: no grid and no scale labels; the zero axis is the vertical line at the start; categories on the left, right aligned, `chart/y-label-gap` from the bar. Donut and `sparkline`: no axis or grid. The axis always starts at zero.
- **Marks**: column and bar corner `chart/bar-radius` at the end only (in a stack, only on the outer segment); between neighbor colors a stroke `chart/segment-gap` in `chart/separator`. Line stroke `chart/line-width`; marker `chart/marker-size` with the series shape; area wash `chart/N-soft` under line N. Colors in fixed order from `chart/1`, never by the active brand and never by situation. Value label (optional, up to 4 series): `chart/data-label-gap` from the bar end, `text/label-sm`, `chart/foreground`.
- **Goal** (optional, from the data): a line `chart/reference-width` in `chart/reference`, `stroke-dasharray` with `chart/reference-dash` and `chart/reference-dash-gap`, label "Goal" at the end of the line, `text/caption`, `chart/label-foreground`.
- **Direct label or legend**: 1 series, none; 2 to 4 series, the direct label as the anatomy says per type, `chart/series-label-gap` from the mark (in the donut, from the box of the ring), `text/caption`, `chart/label-foreground`; above 4 series and in the grouped vertical, the legend below the area, aligned to the start, `chart/section-gap` from it, items `chart/legend-gap` apart, wrapping with `chart/legend-row-gap`. Up to 6 series; from the seventh on, "Others" in `chart/muted`.
- **Legend item**: a button with `aria-pressed` and the series name: padding `chart/legend-item-padding` left and right, height `chart/legend-item-height` (the 24 px target), corner `chart/legend-item-radius`, no background in any state; marker and name `chart/legend-item-gap` apart; name `text/caption` in `chart/legend-item-foreground`, on hover `chart/legend-item-foreground-hover`; with a value (donut), the value at the end, `chart/legend-value-gap` from the name, `text/label-sm`, `chart/foreground`. Hidden: marker hollow (outline `chart/legend-marker-hidden-width` in `chart/legend-marker-hidden`), name and value struck through. Hover of an item puts the other series in `chart/muted`. Focus: border `chart/focus-border` (`chart/focus-border-width`, `chart/legend-item-focus-border-radius`) and halo `chart/focus-halo` (`chart/focus-halo-width`, `chart/legend-item-focus-halo-radius`), outside the item.
- **Data focus**: a ring centered on the focused mark, `chart/focus-offset` apart in `chart/separator`, border `chart/focus-border` (`chart/focus-border-width`, `chart/focus-border-radius`; round `chart/focus-point-radius` around a line marker) and halo `chart/focus-halo` (`chart/focus-halo-width`, `chart/focus-halo-radius`). The border never touches the series color.
- **Data tooltip**: on hover and on data focus, `chart/tooltip-offset` from the data, above by default, turning to the side or below when it does not fit, never cut (DSA-42 D1); background `chart/tooltip-background`, outline `chart/tooltip-border-width` in `chart/tooltip-border`, corner `chart/tooltip-radius`, padding `chart/tooltip-padding`, shadow `elevation/dropdown`; title `text/label-sm` `chart/tooltip-foreground`; one line per visible series: marker with the series shape, name `text/caption` `chart/tooltip-label-foreground`, value `text/label-sm` `chart/tooltip-foreground` aligned to the end; gaps `chart/tooltip-gap`, `chart/tooltip-item-gap`, `chart/tooltip-value-gap`. It appears and leaves by opacity only, in `chart/tooltip-enter-duration` and `chart/tooltip-exit-duration`. WCAG 1.4.13: Esc dismisses it, the pointer can move onto it, and it stays while the pointer or the focus is on the data.
- **Crosshair** (line and area, hover and focus): a continuous vertical line `chart/crosshair-width` in `chart/crosshair`, from the data to the X axis.
- **Highlight** (optional): value `text/heading-lg` `chart/foreground`; `chart/highlight-gap` below it the change: icon `nph-icon` light at `chart/delta-icon-size` (up `arrow-up`; down `arrow-up` turned half a turn until `arrow-down` enters the `nph-icon`; steady `minus`), color by the sense the screen declares (`chart/delta-icon-neutral` default, `chart/delta-icon-positive`, `chart/delta-icon-negative`); number `text/label-sm` and comparison `text/caption`, `chart/delta-gap` apart. The number and the text stay neutral.
- **Donut**: ring diameter `chart/plot-height`, inner radius 75% of the outer; a slice hidden by the legend and the donut tooltip are D-4; total `text/heading-md` and label `text/caption` in the center, `chart/highlight-gap` apart; up to 4 slices, name and percentage outside each slice; 5 or 6 slices, the legend below, one slice per line with the value at the end, in the order of the slices from the top, clockwise.
- **`Sparkline`**: one series, up to 12 values, `chart/line-width`, `chart/sparkline-height` in a table cell; no axis, grid, marker, tooltip or "View as table".
- **States**: loading = `nph-skeleton` block in the area (line in the highlight and the legend), `aria-busy` on the area; empty = `nph-empty` with the heading "No data for the period" and the description "Choose another period to see the data."; no result = `nph-empty` with "No data for this filter" and "Change or clear the filter to see the data."; error = icon `triangle-exclamation` light (`chart/error-icon-size`, `chart/error-icon-foreground`), sentence `text/body-md` `chart/foreground`, `chart/error-gap` between them, and the `nph-button` secondary ghost compact "Try again" `chart/error-stack-gap` below. The card header stays in every state. After "Try again" and after a period change, the area goes through loading while the data comes.
- **No data in the middle of a series**: a column in `chart/empty` with the label "No data" below; in the line, a gap.
- **Keyboard and roles**: the default behavior table of the evidence, with this detail (research of 2026-11-22, below): one Tab stop in the plot (roving tabindex); Left and Right move between the items of a series, Up and Down between the visible series, Home and End to the first and the last item of the series; a hidden series is out of the arrow path. Tab goes on to the legend (each item its own Tab stop) and to "View as table". Enter and Space on a legend item turn its series on and off. The focused data opens its tooltip. Esc closes the tooltip without moving the focus.
- **Accessible name**: the area has a text summary tied to it (WCAG 1.1.1) besides the card title and description; markers and decorative icons `aria-hidden`; state changes announced in a live region.
- **Table alternative**: "View as table" swaps the area for the `nph-table` with the same data and becomes "View as chart"; the focus stays on the button. The root draws, for a column with a goal, Month · Revenue (R$ thousand) · Goal (R$ thousand) · Difference, the difference signed; the column rule is D-3.
- **Long text and many items**: a category label cuts on one line with an ellipsis, the whole text in the tooltip; with 12 categories the X axis shows one label every 2.
- **Motion**: no entry animation of the data.

## Open points for the code
- Event, aria, slot, property and console warning, following the APG and the Custom Elements Manifest.
- **Chart library** (item 12, Pend. 12): the code owner chooses it and gives the estimate the decision map asks for, checking SVG or canvas drawing with an accessible alternative, keyboard on the data, the data table, a tooltip styled with the tokens and the license. Building it in own SVG, with no library, is the alternative. The look and the tokens do not change with the choice. The tokens come out in `rem`; a library that draws on canvas converts `chart/line-width`, `chart/reference-dash` and `chart/reference-dash-gap` to pixels from the root font size.
- **Data API**: how series, categories, values, the unit, the goal, the highlight (value, change, direction, sense, comparison text) and the "Others" grouping reach the chart, and who groups the seventh series on (the component or the consumer). The spec names them.
- **Keyboard in the horizontal types**: in the horizontal bar the categories run vertically; whether the arrows follow the screen (Up and Down between categories) or the model (Left and Right always within the series). MUI X and `Highcharts` do not say on the pages read. The spec says which.
- **Text summary**: the research of 2026-11-22 recommends that the consumer writes the summary through a property (what the data means), and the component builds the rest (chart type, axes, visible series, the link to "View as table"), with a console warning in development when the summary is missing, as `Highcharts` and Cloudscape split it. The spec names the property and the translation keys.
- **Period in the card header**: the `nph-card` spec describes `slot-action` in the base type as the menu action (an icon-only ghost compact `nph-button`) and does not name a period selector. The chart puts there the `nph-toggle-group` compact (up to 3 periods) or, in a narrow card, the `nph-select`. The slot is free and the card does not police it; the spec says how the chart composes with the card (the chart takes the card, or the screen composes the card around the chart) and what width makes the card "narrow".
- **`Sparkline` in a table cell**: the `nph-table` cell types are a closed list and a type outside it draws a blank cell. Showing the `sparkline` in a cell needs a cell type or a slot in the `nph-table`. That is a change of the `nph-table`, with its own task; until then the `sparkline` lives in the card, and the cell case waits. The look does not change.
- **`sparkline` size in a card**: until the table cell exists, the `sparkline` lives in a card: height `chart/sparkline-height` (24), width of its container (120 in Figma is an example width), as the other types follow their container. The spec confirms it.
- **Y-axis scale**: Figma shows 5 labels in round steps from 0 (0, 25, 50, 75, 100) and the axis always starts at zero. How the code picks the top and the step (round numbers) and the number format by locale: the spec names them; the look keeps 5 labels or close to it.
- **Strings and translation keys**: "View as table", "View as chart", "Try again", "Goal", "No data", "Others", the empty and no-result headings and descriptions (the four texts in "States"), the sentence of the error and, if D-2 is followed, "No series selected". Number and date format by locale.
- **D-1 to D-4**: negative values, every series hidden, the table columns and the donut with a hidden slice, decisions of the design owner; until she answers, the code follows the recommendation written in each.
