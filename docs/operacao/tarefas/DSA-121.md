# DSA-121 — nph-sidebar background: neutral (default) or brand

## Goal
The menu reads lighter: by default its panel is neutral, white in light and the card surface in
dark, and the brand color stays on the details (the active item, the counter and the corner
button). The panel in the brand color stays available as `background="brand"`, for the product
that asks for the menu in its brand color. The collapse icon and the mobile close X turn gray on
the neutral panel.

## What changed in the tokens (on v/5.0.0 in the same push as this task)
| Token | Points to (light / dark) | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| `sidebar/panel-background` | `color/card` / `color/card` (white `#ffffff` / `core/slate/800`) | **Now the neutral panel background.** Before, it pointed to `color/primary-surface-hover`. |
| `sidebar/panel-border` | `core/alpha/black-0` / `color/border-subtle` | **Now the neutral panel outline:** transparent in light (no semantic token has the role of a transparent outline, so it points to core, T9 rule 3); in dark the soft outline, because there the elevation comes from the surface and the border. Before, it pointed to `theme/brand-200` / `theme/brand-700`. |
| `sidebar/panel-background-brand` (new name) | `color/primary-surface-hover` | The brand panel background: the value `sidebar/panel-background` had before. |
| `sidebar/panel-border-brand` (new name) | `theme/brand-200` / `theme/brand-700` | The brand panel outline: the value `sidebar/panel-border` had before. |
| `sidebar/toggle-foreground` (new) | `color/subtle-foreground` (`core/slate/500` / `core/slate/200`) | Icon of the collapse button and of the mobile close X, neutral background. |
| `sidebar/toggle-foreground-brand` (new) | `color/primary-on-surface` | The same icons, brand background. It is the color `.toggle` paints today. |

CSS variables: `--nph-sidebar-panel-background`, `--nph-sidebar-panel-border`,
`--nph-sidebar-panel-background-brand`, `--nph-sidebar-panel-border-brand`,
`--nph-sidebar-toggle-foreground`, `--nph-sidebar-toggle-foreground-brand`. The shadows are the
existing styles `elevation/raised` (`--nph-elevation-raised`) and `elevation/drawer`
(`--nph-elevation-drawer`), with no component token. `shadow/color` is transparent in dark, so in
dark neither panel has a shadow without any rule of its own.

**Between the token push and this task.** The two names without suffix changed value, and the
code still reads them for every menu. Until this task lands, v/5.0.0 draws every `nph-sidebar`
with the neutral background and outline, but with no shadow and with the brand-colored collapse
icon. No test turns red: the tests read the custom properties, not the colors.

## Figma documentation (accepted), as text
**Set:** `nph-sidebar` (`4605:9647`), documentation page `4633:27036`. The set has the property
`fundo = neutro | marca`; the default variant is `fundo=neutro, modo=expandido, estado=pronto`. The
brand variants keep their ids, so the instances that existed keep the brand look in Figma.

**Description.** The neutral background is the default: the panel is white in light and the card
surface in dark, and the brand color shows on the details (active item, counter and corner
button). The brand background paints the panel in the active brand color, for the product that
asks for the menu in the brand color.

**Anatomy, 1. Panel.** Background `sidebar/panel-background`, 1 px border in
`sidebar/panel-border` and radius `sidebar/panel-radius` (collapsed: `sidebar/rail-radius`). It
rises from the page with `elevation/raised` (mobile: `elevation/drawer`); in dark the shadow goes
away, and the card surface and the soft border separate the panel from the page. With the brand
background: `sidebar/panel-background-brand` and `sidebar/panel-border-brand`, no shadow.

**Anatomy, 3. Collapse button.** Primary ghost icon button next to the logo (below the logo mark
when collapsed). The icon is `sidebar` (Font Awesome, light), the same to collapse and to expand,
with the state in `aria-label` and `aria-expanded`, in `sidebar/toggle-foreground` (gray); with the
brand background, `sidebar/toggle-foreground-brand`. Size `icon/size-md` (20 × 20). In the mobile
mode the same place holds the close button (`xmark`), in the same color.

**Anatomy, 8. Corner button (optional).** When the Figma boolean `botão do canto` is off, the whole
foot is hidden, so the navigation takes its room.

**Properties.**
| Figma | Code | What changes |
|---|---|---|
| `fundo` | `background` | `neutro` (default): white panel, no outline, `elevation/raised` (mobile `elevation/drawer`); in dark, card surface with the soft outline and no shadow; collapse and close icons in gray. `marca`: the panel in the active brand color, with the brand border and no shadow; collapse and close icons in the brand color. |

**When to use.** "The product asks for the menu in the brand color": `background="brand"`; in every
other case the neutral background, the default, stays.

**Do.** Use the neutral background by default; leave the brand color for the details: the panel
stays light, and the active item and the corner button stand out.

**Relations.** The product chooses the background, the mode, the current item and what opens; the
menu only shows. The panel, the search background and border, the separator, the items, the group
label and the collapse and close icons use the `sidebar/*` tokens; the corner button uses the
pair of the primary solid `nph-button`, and the focus, `focus/border`.

**Contrast (the lowest of the seven brands, measured in Figma).**
- Text: every text of the menu passes 4.5:1 on its background, in both themes and on both
  backgrounds. In the `sistemas` theme the lowest is 5.37:1 light and 6.93:1 dark: the label of the
  corner button, which does not change with the background. Across all brands the lowest is 4.96:1
  (the corner button label, `educacao`, light).
- Collapse and close icons: 5.85:1 light and 8.20:1 dark at least on the neutral panel; 4.26:1 and
  7.68:1 on the brand panel. They are icons of controls, so they need 3:1 (WCAG 1.4.11).
- Focus: `focus/border` on every focusable piece of the menu (item, subitem, rail button, flyout
  option, collapse and close buttons). Against the neutral panel at least 4.32:1 light and 6.12:1
  dark; against the brand panel 3.38:1 and 6.74:1; against the flyout panel 4.32:1 and 7.43:1. The
  border meets 3:1; the halo is a reinforcement. See the open point about the `sistemas` theme below.
- The active item, the open group, the flyout title and the active option use `color/primary-text`,
  which measures 4.5:1 on the neutral panel, on the brand panel and on the flyout panel.

## Default behavior
The background property changes only paint. Keyboard, focus on open and on close, Esc, click
outside, the scrim, the position and the motion of the mobile menu do not change. Event, aria,
slot, property and console warning stay with the code (Elvys), following the APG and the Custom
Elements Manifest.

Follows DSA-53 D-2 (the counter outline in the panel color) and DSA-72 D-1 (the collapse button
draws the `sidebar` icon).

## Open point (does not block this task)
**`focus/border` in the `sistemas` theme.** Figma and v/5.0.0 differ on `theme/brand-focus` for
`sistemas`: Figma points to `core/sistemas/600` (`#2f68c5`, 5.37:1 on white) and
`src/tokens/source/theme.tokens.json` (`brand-focus`, mode `sistemas`) to `core.sistemas.500`
(`#3b82f6`, 3.68:1 on white). Both pass 3:1 on the neutral panel, so this task keeps `focus/border`
as v/5.0.0 has it. Which tone is right is not decided here: owner `indiane`; the answer comes as a
token change in another push.

## How it is proved
**`review-and-merge`** passes when the tests of `nph-sidebar` and `nph-sidebar-rail-button` are
green with both backgrounds (panel colors, shadow, toggle and close icon colors, the counter
outline, the foot hidden with the corner button off), the spec, the metadata, the docs page and the
comments follow, the proof commands in `docs/operacao/component-handoff.md` exit 0, and the change
is merged into v/5.0.0.

## What this task does not do
It does not change the items, subitems, rail buttons or flyout panel colors (`sidebar/item-*`,
`sidebar/rail-*`, `sidebar/flyout-*`), the separator (`sidebar/separator`, which stays on
`color/primary-surface`), the search (`sidebar/search-*`; DSA-119 changes its border), nor the
corner button. DSA-117 and DSA-119 also edit `nph-sidebar.css` and `nph-sidebar.test.ts`; the line
numbers above are those of v/5.0.0 when this task was opened.
