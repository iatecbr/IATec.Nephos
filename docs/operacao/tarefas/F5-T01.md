## What this task does not do
It delivers the directory and the convention, and nothing beyond that. **It does not
document any block:** `fichas/_modelo.md`, in §5, and `README.md`, in the Taxonomy,
require that block, layout and template only be documented after being extracted from
a real pattern or an approved mock — and the M5 pilot does not depend on Figma, a mock
or visual approval.

It does not register, edit or depend on content, decision, evidence or a component
spec. It does not touch Figma, `design.md`, tokens, `src/`, the rendered Storybook or
`docs/stories.md`. It does not deliver the development guide, which is F4-T04.
