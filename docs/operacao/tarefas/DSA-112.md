# DSA-112 — `nph-toolbar`: the split button inside the bar

## Goal
The `nph-toolbar` puts its actions in one Tab stop, moves between them with Left and Right Arrow (wrapping, with Home and End) and writes the size on the direct children it lists (`fichas/nph-toolbar.md`, business rules and keyboard; P104, items 3 and 5). It does not know a piece with two actions. The design owner decided that, inside the bar, **each part of the `nph-split-button` is an action of the bar**: the side arrows go from the main part to the arrow, and Down Arrow on the arrow opens its menu (item 10).

## Where the decision is
In the evidence of the `foundation-decision` gate: `docs/operacao/evidencias/DSA-112/foundation-decision-2026-11-23.md`. There is no need to open Figma.

## What changes
- The roving focus of the bar reaches the two parts of the split as two actions, in order: main part, then arrow. A disabled part is skipped, as any disabled action; a loading main part is still an action.
- On the arrow, Down Arrow opens the menu, as the APG menu button; inside the menu, the menu keys rule, and Esc gives the focus back to the arrow, which stays the current action of the bar.
- The bar writes its size on the split, which writes it on both parts.
- The caveat recorded with the decision (in a vertical bar, Down Arrow on the arrow competes with the navigation) does not apply in this version: the `nph-toolbar` has no vertical bar (`fichas/nph-toolbar.md`).
- The spec, the metadata and `docs/decisoes-tecnicas.md` (P104) record the rule.

## How it is proved
**`foundation-decision`** — passed. **`review-and-merge`** — a test with a split in the bar (arrows across the two parts, Down Arrow opening the menu, Esc back to the arrow, a disabled part skipped); the proof commands in `docs/operacao/component-handoff.md` exit 0; merged into `v/5.0.0`.

## Why it is blocked
It needs the `nph-split-button` (DSA-109) in the code.

## What this task does not do
It does not create a token. It does not build the split (DSA-109). It does not change Figma.
