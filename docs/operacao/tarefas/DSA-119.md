# DSA-119 — Form fields with a split border and height 38

## Goal
A form field at rest reads lighter: the top and the sides of its border are soft, and only the
bottom line is strong. The bottom line is the one that meets 3:1 for the boundary of the field
(WCAG 1.4.11). The default height of the fields goes from 36 to 38; button, icon button and the
other controls stay at 36. The arrow area of the select-like fields loses its background and its
divider.

## What changed in the tokens (on v/5.0.0 in the same push as this task)
Values read variable by variable from the Figma file `DS-IA-NEPHOS 5.0` (`VariableID:8313:28049`
to `8313:28070`). `color/background` is `#ffffff` in light and `core/slate/900` in dark.

| Token | Points to | Light | Dark |
|---|---|---|---|
| `core/control-height/field` (new) | 38 px | 38 | 38 |
| `control/height-field` (new) | `core/control-height/field` | 38 | 38 |
| `color/field-border` (new) | `core/slate/200` · `core/slate/700` | `#bfc6d1`, 1.72:1 on `color/background` | `#323e4e`, 1.58:1 |
| `color/field-border-bottom` (new) | `core/slate/300` · `core/slate/400` | `#8896aa`, 3.00:1 on `color/background` | `#6f7d91`, 4.09:1 |
| `input/border`, `select/border`, `combobox/border`, `multi-combobox/border`, `textarea/border`, `date-picker/border` (new) | `color/field-border` | as above | as above |
| `input/border-bottom`, `select/border-bottom`, `combobox/border-bottom`, `multi-combobox/border-bottom`, `textarea/border-bottom`, `date-picker/border-bottom` (new) | `color/field-border-bottom` | as above | as above |

| `pagination/field-height` (new) | `control/height-default` | 36 | 36 |

The CSS variables are `--nph-<piece>-border`, `--nph-<piece>-border-bottom`, `--nph-<piece>-height`
and `--nph-pagination-field-height`. `textarea` has no height token: its height is still
`layout/textarea-min-height` and `layout/textarea-max-height`. `control/height-default`,
`control/height-compact`, `control/height-large`, `input/height-compact`, `color/input` and
`color/input-hover` keep their values. Only their descriptions in `design.md` changed:
`color/input` is now the full outline of checkbox, radio, the `nph-input-otp` cell, the
`nph-pagination` arrows and the disabled field; `color/input-hover` is the whole border of a field
in hover; `control/height-default` is button and the other controls.

## Figma documentation (accepted), as text

### Border by state
The split border exists only at rest. Every other state draws the whole border in one color, as
today.

| Figma state | Top and sides | Bottom line | Sizes |
|---|---|---|---|
| `default` (rest) | `<piece>/border` | `<piece>/border-bottom` | compact (input only), default and large |
| `foco-limpar` (focus on the clear button inside the field) | `<piece>/border` | `<piece>/border-bottom` | all |
| `leitura` (read-only `nph-textarea`) | `textarea/border` | `textarea/border-bottom` | — |
| `hover` | `color/input-hover`, whole | same | all |
| `foco` and open list | `focus/border`, whole, with the `focus/halo` outside | same | all |
| `erro` and `erro-foco` | `status/error`, whole | same | all |
| `disabled` | `color/input`, whole, on `color/muted` | same | all |

In CSS, the border width stays `border/width` on all four sides, so the box does not move between
states, and the bottom line is the bottom border: `border-color: var(--nph-<piece>-border);
border-block-end-color: var(--nph-<piece>-border-bottom);`. Do not use `box-shadow` for it
(`design.md`, `regras_sombra_interna`). The Figma master is drawn another way, because a Figma
stroke has one color only: the stroke is `<piece>/border` and the bottom line is a 1 px inner
shadow in `<piece>/border-bottom`. The "four sides" rule is for the CSS, not for the master.

`nph-tree-select` has no `tree-select/border*` or `tree-select/height` token. By decision, as in
Figma, where its field is an instance of `nph-select` (single), `nph-combobox` (single with
search) or `nph-multi-combobox` (multiple), it reads the tokens of that piece. This is a declared
exception to rule T9 ("the component uses only its own token").

### Arrow area (`nph-select`, `nph-combobox`, `nph-tree-select`)
"No background and no divider, at the end of the trigger. The arrow points down with the list
closed and up with the list open." The area is `control/height-default` wide in default (36) and `control/height-large` in large
(44), with no divider: today the box is 37 (36 plus the 1 px divider), after this task it is 36,
and the text gains that pixel. Its height is the field height minus the two borders: 36 in
default (38 − 2) and 42 in large. It stays part of the click target. In disabled it shows the
field background (`color/muted`), because it has no background of its own. The Figma masters keep
a hidden layer named `divisória`; it is not drawn. The arrow keeps 3:1 on the field background.

### Contrast (WCAG 1.4.11)
The bottom line meets 3:1 against the background: 3.00:1 in light and 4.09:1 in dark on
`color/background`. The top and the sides are lighter on purpose. The text and the placeholder
keep 4.5:1. The disabled state is exempt. The halo is decoration; the focus border is
`focus/border`.

## Default behavior
Keyboard, focus on open and on close, Esc, click outside, pointer and position near the edge of
the window do not change: each piece keeps the behavior of its own task. The pointer only paints
the border (hover); it does not move the focus. The combined states keep the order they have
today: disabled wins over everything; read-only (`nph-textarea`) keeps the resting border under
the pointer and shows the focus; error keeps `status/error` in hover and in focus; focus and open
list take `focus/border`; focus on a button inside the field (clear, remove a chip) keeps the
resting split border, also under the pointer; then hover; then rest.

Event, aria, slot, property and console warning stay with the code (Elvys), following the APG and
the Custom Elements Manifest.

Follows DSA-29 D-1 (the disabled arrow area shows the field background) and DSA-95 (the option
list outline is `color/border-subtle`; this task does not touch it).

## Open point (does not block this task)
The `nph-sidebar` search sits on `sidebar/search-background` (`color/primary-surface`, the
brand-50 tone in light). Its bottom line, `input/border-bottom` (`core/slate/300`), measures

task implements what the accepted Figma draws; a change, if any, comes as a new token from Figma
in another task.
