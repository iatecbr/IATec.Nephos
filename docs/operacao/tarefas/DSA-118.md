# DSA-118 — nph-pagination and nph-toolbar without the outer border

## Goal
The bar of `nph-pagination` and the bar of `nph-toolbar` no longer draw an outline around
their content. The pieces inside keep their own borders (arrows, previous and next buttons,
search field). The floating toolbar keeps its background, radius and shadow
(`elevation/dropdown`), without the border.

## What changed in Figma (already done, file DS IA NEPHOS 5.0)
| Component | Variants without the outer border | Figma size |
|---|---|---|
| `nph-pagination` (set `1640:194`) | `tipo=numerada` (3), `tipo=com extremos` (3) and the inner bar of `tipo=tabela` (3) | 556 × 52 and 516 × 52 (were 558 × 54 and 518 × 54: the border was drawn inside) |
| `nph-toolbar` (set `2105:426`) | `tipo=página` (3), `tipo=ferramentas` (3) and `tipo=flutuante` (3) | unchanged (the border was drawn outside) |

The other variants already had no outer border: pagination simple, range, compact; toolbar bulk
and applied filters.

**Test that fails if the list is kept.** `nph-pagination.test.ts:895`–`901` expects every name of
its list to be read by the CSS (`expect(used).toContain(name)`). Once the border leaves the CSS,
the two border names must leave the list, or the test goes red. `nph-toolbar` has the same kind
of test at `nph-toolbar.test.ts:953`–`958` (see the table).

**Historical evidence stays.** `docs/operacao/evidencias/DSA-54/` and `DSA-55/` name the border
tokens; they record what was approved then and are not edited.

## What this task does not do
It does not change the tokens. `pagination/border`, `pagination/border-width`,
`toolbar/border`, `toolbar/border-width` and `toolbar/floating-border` stay in
`src/tokens/generated/tokens.css` (lines 905, 914, 1323, 1329 and 1331); after this task no
component CSS reads them. It does not change the arrows of `nph-pagination`
(`pagination/arrow-border`), the focus ring, the separators between toolbar groups, nor the
pieces inside the bars.

## How it is proved
**`review-and-merge`** passes when `npx vitest run src/components/nph-pagination src/components/nph-toolbar`
is green, the computed style of each `.bar` has no border (pagination; toolbar page, tools and
floating), the floating bar still has the `elevation/dropdown` shadow, the specs, the metadata
and the Storybook texts no longer name the outer border, the proof commands in
`docs/operacao/component-handoff.md` exit 0, and the change is merged into `v/5.0.0`.
